Delete your account data

  1. Open Zenith and select the Profile button.
  2. Confirm you are signed in with Apple.
  3. Under Account, select Delete account.
  4. Review the ownership warning and confirm deletion.

This permanently deletes local properties, vehicles, documents, reminders, preferences, and stored Apple identity. Owned CloudKit resources are deleted for all participants; resources shared with you remain with their owners and your access is removed.

Cloud sharing troubleshooting

Confirm that the device is signed into iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and Zenith has permission to use iCloud. Invitations and deletion should be tested on physical devices using the intended Apple Accounts.
